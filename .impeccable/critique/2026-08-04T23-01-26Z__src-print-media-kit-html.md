## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment:** This does not read as generic AI-made editorial design. The deep green-charcoal field, stacked EL / BRI / EFF lockup, portrait placement, and restrained green accent deliberately match the documented El Brieff identity. The weak signals are the repeated tracked 8pt labels and non-informational 02 / 03 markers, but they do not dominate.

**Deterministic scan:** The static scan returned 6 `overused-font` warnings at lines 66, 162, 246, 299, 338, and 410. These are false positives: Montserrat is the committed display and label typeface in DESIGN.md. Browser detection reported undersized 8pt labels, three “side-tab accent border” targets, overused font, monotonous spacing, and one overlay-induced occlusion warning. The side-tab findings are false positives because the rules are full-width `border-top`s; the occlusion finding is generated by the detector itself. The 8pt label finding is relevant only for on-screen reading, not necessarily for this A4 print system.

**Visual overlays:** The detector injected successfully into a new browser tab at `http://localhost:8765/src/print/media-kit.html`; it reported 10 targets and 11 individual warnings with no console errors. The decisive browser evidence is separate from the overlay: in print media, page 2 has a 1123px visible frame but a 1526px scroll height, so its lower content is clipped.

## Overall Impression

The first page establishes a confident, recognizable El Brieff press identity. The kit then fails on the job that matters most: page 2 can export with missing content, and page 3 makes the next press action unnecessarily manual and diffuse. Stabilize the printed artifact first; then make the final page a clear conversion surface.

## What's Working

- The cover uses the brand’s actual visual grammar: dark green-charcoal ground, white stacked wordmark, Brieffy signature, and an editorial portrait with a direct promise.
- The story order is sensible: what El Brieff is, who leads it, then resources and contact. It avoids unsupported audience metrics, awards, or social-proof claims.
- Source semantics and interaction basics are careful: Spanish document language, descriptive portrait alt text, headings, real anchor text, and visible keyboard focus styles.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Page 2 content is clipped in print

**Why it matters:** The print stylesheet locks each page to `height: 297mm` with `overflow: hidden` (lines 647–656). Browser print evidence measured page 2 at 1526px tall inside a 1123px frame. The host portrait is rendered at 180 × 1000px, contradicting the intended `aspect-ratio: 4 / 5` and driving the overflow. The biography, facts, and/or footer can be silently cut off in the PDF or printer output.

**Fix:** Correct the host-image sizing with an explicit bounded height or an image wrapper, then verify all three pages in actual print preview. Do not ship fixed-height pages until every page’s print scroll height equals its page height.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`

### [P1] Page 3 has no frictionless action for a printed press kit

**Why it matters:** Assets, five listening platforms, email, and the site appear only as links (lines 793–870). A person holding an A4 sheet cannot act without manually typing addresses; five equal platform choices add decision load precisely at the conversion point.

**Fix:** Make a verified press hub the one primary action. Add a print-safe short URL and QR code for the hub, plus one optional QR for “Escuchar.” Move the five services behind a single “Todas las plataformas” destination.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

### [P1] The cover reconstructs, rather than uses, the canonical cover asset

**Why it matters:** DESIGN.md identifies `elbrieff-cover.png` as the canonical brand asset, but the cover recreates the lockup around a separate portrait (lines 672–686). That invites visual drift between the press kit, platforms, and social assets.

**Fix:** Use the canonical cover directly, or document and approve this press-kit composition as a distinct controlled lockup with a high-resolution master.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`

### [P1] Image and font delivery are brittle for export and deployment

**Why it matters:** Font imports reach directly into `../../node_modules` (lines 11–15). This worked under the local server, but production/export must expose and embed those paths. The cover portrait also renders at about 141ppi at its final print size, below a premium 300ppi print target.

**Fix:** Bundle or self-host font files in a public asset path and verify a fresh production build/PDF embeds them. Replace the cover image with a print master at least 2185px high for its final size, preferably 300ppi.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

### [P2] The resources page lacks a clear press-first hierarchy

**Why it matters:** The reader sees three separate assets, five listening destinations, an email, a site link, and four brand rules with no declared primary task. The note that an asset package will arrive later makes the approved route feel unfinished.

**Fix:** Lead with “Descargar kit de prensa” (or the press hub), follow with contact and use terms, and demote listening platforms to secondary reference material. Use “02 / 03” only if page navigation is a real user need.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable distill`

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (first-timer):** On page 3, “Archivos oficiales” offers three equal files while a note says the bundle is not yet available. Jordan has no clear answer to “what do I download now?”

**Sam (accessibility-dependent):** Semantic structure and focus styles are good, but `dt` labels are 7.5pt (lines 337–343), which is fragile for low-vision print reading. A static PDF/printout has no non-link equivalent of the asset and listening actions.

**Casey (distracted mobile user):** Touch targets are adequate, but the mobile route still asks Casey to choose among five services. The hero image and portrait load eagerly, while the desired next action is not consolidated or positioned for a fast, one-handed decision.

## Minor Observations

- `download` on the three cross-origin asset URLs may be ignored unless remote response headers explicitly permit attachment downloads.
- The manual date/version footer appears twice and can drift.
- The 4px top rules are aligned with the brand accent but should not become repeated default scaffolding across future pages.
- Office printers may add white margins or suppress the dark cover background; choose a tested PDF workflow and offer a printer-safe variant if the kit is meant for home/office output.
